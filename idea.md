Wolf - Management


1. This app will be my management software. Management for everything not only business. 
2. From research to project, everything will be handled here.
3. Everything will be managed as a project or research or academic. 
4. So say I am doing a research i will create a new entity of a category (project, research, academic)
5. Every category will have tasks and task there could be subtasks. 
6. Every project, resarch or academic will have a thumbnail as business in the dashboard. 
7. In research, There will be a completion steps on the top. Like literature review, idea generation to paper writing etc. 
8. For project or academics, I can also create a step line like this if i want to.
